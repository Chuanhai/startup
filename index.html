Smart Passport Photo Support

Support for the Smart Passport Photo iPhone app.

How the app works

Smart Passport Photo helps you capture, prepare, preview, and check a U.S. passport or visa-style digital photo on your iPhone.

You can preview the photo and automated checks before purchasing. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks the current digital photo for saving.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Where is my photo processed?

Photo processing is performed on your device. The app does not require you to upload your photo to a developer-operated server for processing.

Does the app guarantee that my photo will be accepted?

No. Automated checks are guidance only. Final acceptance is determined by the U.S. Department of State or the relevant reviewing authority.

How does payment work?

Purchases are processed through Apple’s App Store using StoreKit. The app does not directly collect or store your payment-card information.

How do I save my photo?

After purchase, tap Save My Photo. You may be asked to allow access to Photos so the image can be saved to your device.

I purchased but cannot save the photo. What should I do?

Confirm that the app has permission to add photos in iPhone Settings, then reopen the app and try again. If the issue continues, contact support below.
