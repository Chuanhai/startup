Privacy Policy

For Smart Passport Photo

Smart Passport Photo is designed to prepare passport- and visa-style digital photos on your iPhone. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Photo Processing

Photos captured or selected for use in Smart Passport Photo are processed on your device. The app does not require you to upload your photos to a developer-operated server for photo processing.

Camera and Photo Library Access

The app may request access to your camera so you can take a photo. It may also request permission to save a completed photo to your Photos library. These permissions are used only to provide the requested app functionality.

Accounts

Smart Passport Photo does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The developer does not directly receive or store your full payment-card information. Apple may process purchase-related information according to Apple’s own privacy policies and terms.

Data Collection

Based on the current version of the app, the developer does not intentionally collect photos, names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, or other personal information through a developer-operated backend.

Third-Party Services

The current app uses Apple platform services such as camera/photo APIs and StoreKit. If additional analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, cloud, or third-party SDKs are added in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated as appropriate.

Children’s Privacy

The app is not specifically directed to children and does not require account registration.

Data Retention

Photos saved to your device remain under your control in your Photos library. Any temporary app data used during photo preparation is handled locally on the device.

Government Affiliation

Smart Passport Photo is an independent photo-preparation tool and is not affiliated with the U.S. government. Automated photo checks are provided as guidance only, and final acceptance is determined by the relevant reviewing authority.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. The latest version should be made available at the same public URL.
